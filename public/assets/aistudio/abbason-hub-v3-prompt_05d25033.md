## Business rules (these replace earlier rules where they differ)

**Money (unchanged).** All amounts are computed on the SERVER only, in naira, and sent to Paystack in kobo. `fee(x)` = the existing `calculateGatewayFee`.
- Delivery fee: buyer pays `deliveryFee + fee(deliveryFee)`, split to the seller's subaccount with `transaction_charge = fee(deliveryFee)`, `bearer: 'account'`. The seller receives the full delivery fee; the platform takes 0%.
- Goods: buyer pays `goods + fee(goods)`. `commission = round(0.03 x goods)`. `transaction_charge = commission + fee(goods)`, `bearer: 'account'`. The seller receives `goods - commission`.
- Vendor fee: `vendorFee + fee(vendorFee)`, NO subaccount. 2,000 for the first 20 paying vendors, then 5,000.

**Payment marks delivery.** There is no "Delivered" button for sellers. An item counts as delivered when the buyer pays for it in the app. The buyer can also reject a faulty item (existing reject route, reason required): no goods payment, and the delivery fee is not refunded.

**Phone numbers and contact.** Every seller must have a saved Nigerian mobile number before they can list anything. Both sides see each other's number, name and location, but only after the order is "committed":
- Delivery with a fee: committed when the delivery fee is paid.
- Pickup, and delivery with a fee of 0: committed as soon as the order is placed.

The server sets `contactShared: true` on the order at that moment. Remove the old "reveal 48 hours before the deadline" rule and the field `contactRevealAtMs` completely.

**Delivery orders (seller delivers).** The buyer gives a Nigerian mobile number, a delivery location (safe checkpoint from the campus list, or typed text of 3 to 120 characters) and a preferred day within the next 7 days. The checkout says: "Your phone number and location are shared with the seller once you pay the delivery fee. The seller will call you to agree a delivery time." The seller calls the buyer, can type an optional "Agreed time" (max 60 characters) that the buyer sees on the order, delivers, and the buyer inspects and pays for each item in the app.

**Pickup orders (buyer collects).** Every listing that allows pickup MUST have a pickup location (a safe checkpoint from the campus list, OR typed text of 3 to 120 characters), one or more pickup days of the week, a time slot (morning, afternoon, evening, any) and an optional note (max 60 characters). A listing can allow delivery, pickup or both, at least one. The buyer picks one of the next matching pickup dates (tomorrow to 7 days ahead, Africa/Lagos). Public pages show the pickup days and time slot, and the location only if it is a safe checkpoint; a typed location shows "Exact location shown after you order". After the order is placed both sides see each other's number and the buyer sees the exact location, date and time. The buyer collects, inspects and pays in the app.

**Free delivery is treated exactly like pickup** (no upfront payment, same limits, same rule P4).

**Cancelling.** The buyer may cancel until 48 hours before the deadline (`cancelCutoffMs = deadline - 48h`). The delivery fee is not refunded. After that cancelling is closed. The checkout must show "You can cancel until <date and time>" or "You will not be able to cancel this order" when the deadline is less than 48 hours away.

**Pay inside the app.** Checkout and the goods-payment screen both show: "Always pay inside the app. Paying outside the app means no refund and can get your account closed."

**Penalty rules**
- **P1 Off-app payment.** "Asked me to pay outside the app" is a reason in the existing complaint form (buyer and seller). It lands in the admin complaints queue. If I confirm a seller took a payment outside the app, I use the new "Remove seller permanently" button (Phase C). Nothing happens automatically to a seller.
- **P2 Delivery orders with a fee, seller does not deliver.** Unchanged: if the order is still unpaid-for and not rejected 24 hours after the deadline, the seller gets a strike (`sellerDefault`) and owes 2x the delivery fee (this includes giving back the fee) by bank transfer outside the app. The server only flags it; I decide the account action. A seller's "buyer refused to pay or did not show up" report overrides this.
- **P3 Seller report.** The seller's existing "Buyer refused to pay" report gets a second reason, "Buyer did not show up" (reason required). Both go to the admin queue and the existing "Ban buyer for 30 days" button.
- **P4 Pickup and free-delivery orders, buyer ghosts.** 24 hours after the deadline, if the order is still `scheduled` with nothing paid, no item rejected, not cancelled by the buyer, there is NO seller report on it, and the buyer has NOT filed a complaint on it, then the server suspends the BUYER for 30 days (`bannedUntilMs`), cancels the order and restores the stock. The suspended buyer sees a notice for the whole 30 days: "Your account is suspended until <date>. Please explain what happened" with a text box (max 1000 characters). I see the explanation in the admin queue and can press "Lift ban" or "Keep ban". If the seller reports the buyer (rule P3), nothing automatic happens and the case goes to my queue.
- **P4b Pickup and free-delivery orders, buyer says the seller did not answer or show up.** The buyer has a button "Seller did not answer / did not show up" on these orders (a reason in the existing complaint form). When it is used, nobody is suspended and nobody gets a strike: no buyer ban, no seller strike, no admin case. The only thing that happens is that the order is cancelled and the stock restored when its deadline is reached (or straight away if the deadline has already passed), and the order is marked `cancelReason: 'seller_unreachable'`. Nothing else. (This applies to pickup and free-delivery orders only. Delivery orders with a paid fee keep rule P2.)
- **P5 Limit.** A buyer can have at most 2 pickup or free-delivery orders at the same time that are `scheduled` with nothing paid (`MAX_OPEN_UNPAID_ORDERS = 2`).

**Constants** (shared config file used by client and server): `CANCEL_CUTOFF_HOURS = 48`, `SELLER_GRACE_HOURS = 24`, `BUYER_GHOST_GRACE_HOURS = 24`, `BAN_DAYS = 30`, `MAX_DELIVERY_DAYS = 7`, `PENALTY_MULTIPLIER = 2`, `COMMISSION_RATE = 0.03`, `MAX_OPEN_UNPAID_ORDERS = 2`, `COLLECTION_WARN = 6000000`, `COLLECTION_RED = 7500000`, and the vendor fee constants.
